# ShedLink CORE standalone — compliant replacement ideas

**Independent brainstorm by Jim — 2026-08-26**

## Design filter

Every idea below is designed for the mobile 13+ surface: no wager, no paid entry leading to a prize, no system-random valuable outcome, no lootbox/case/rarity reveal, no near-miss, and no cashable or transferable reward. Crustики (💎) are earned in CORE only. Paid ideas mean a fixed 💎 price for an exact, previewed result.

I did not re-propose P2P market/trading/transfers, randomized crafting/forging, cases under a new name, casino wheels/raffles, or expansions of family/guild/pet systems (explicitly paused in `DEFERRED.md:28-49`). The goal is a module-independent identity and activity loop, consistent with the roadmap’s persistent identity/progression/relationships direction (`ROADMAP.md:43-50`) and full Free core experience (`ROADMAP.md:804-807`).

Effort is rough end-to-end product effort: **S** small, **M** medium, **L** large.

## Recommended product bundle

The highest-leverage standalone CORE is not one replacement but a compact loop:

1. **Quest Routes** tell viewers what to do now.
2. **Signal Sprint / Tic-Tac-Toe Tactics** provide immediate skill play.
3. **Channel Expedition** converts individual activity into a social shared goal.
4. **Shed Passport + Mastery Tracks** preserve progress across streams.
5. **Exact Workshop** gives earned 💎 a compliant, deterministic sink.
6. **Glory Circuit** supplies competition without entry fees or valuable prizes.

This satisfies the four lost itches without cosmetic relabeling:

| Cut itch | Replacement |
|---|---|
| Quick 1v1 thrill | Signal Sprint, Codebreaker Duels, Tic-Tac-Toe Tactics |
| Collect/surprise/rarity dopamine | Visible Shed Passport, Mastery Tracks, Community Mosaic |
| Compete for glory | Free Glory Circuit with titles/Hall of Fame |
| Spend for progress | Exact Workshop and Loadout Builder |

## Compliance rules to bake into acceptance tests

- Mobile UI contains no `case`, loot, drop odds, rarity roll, chest/open/reveal, dice-as-randomizer, jackpot, wager, pot, bet, or paid-entry prize language.
- Every 💎 button names the exact price and exact outcome before confirmation.
- Skill competition entry is free; any recognition reward and tiebreak rules are published in advance.
- Fixed daily/weekly schedules are shared across users; do not randomize challenge difficulty or reward per viewer.
- Correct trivia/prediction rewards every qualifying participant identically; never choose a random winner.
- Cosmetics/profile items are nontransferable and cannot be sold back or converted to money/Bits.
- Hidden mechanics are not merely CSS-hidden; they are absent from the mobile DOM and inaccessible through mobile navigation.
